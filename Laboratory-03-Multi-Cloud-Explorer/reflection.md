2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?

All three platforms share core Infrastructure-as-a-Service (IaaS) capabilities, including Virtual Machines, Object Storage, Relational Databases, and Virtual Private Networks. Furthermore, each provider structures its global infrastructure around fault-tolerant Regions and Availability Zones while securing resources through granular Identity and Access Management (IAM) tools.
___________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?

Business requirements directly dictate cloud selection because technical choices must align with an organization's specific budget, existing technology stack, and scaling goals. For instance, a university relying on Microsoft 365 benefits most from Azure, whereas an AI research firm requires GCP's high-performance computing hardware, and a growing startup needs AWS's cost-effective scalability.

4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to evaluate cloud infrastructure through a strategic business lens rather than focusing solely on technical specs. I learned that an effective cloud architect does not simply pick the most popular platform, but instead analyzes unique client constraints to recommend the provider that best solves the business problem.
